PawFriend - Habit Tracking App 

Description

PawFriend is a full-stack habit tracking web application with a virtual pet system that motivates users through gamification by turning habit-building into a cozy adventure. Complete your habits to grow your virtual puppy, earn experience, unlock accessories, and build streaks. 

## PawFriend was built as a final year project for **KV6027 Computing Practical Project** at Northumbria University. ##

About the Project

Most habit trackers feel clinical and forgettable. PawFriend takes a different approach: instead of tracking habits for yourself, you're caring for a virtual puppy whose mood and growth depend on whether you keep your daily commitments. 
The pet evolves through three life stages Puppy → Teen → Adult and reflects three moods (sad, normal, happy) based on how many of the day's habits you've completed.
The result is a habit tracker that is genuinely motivating, friendly, and fun to come back to.

Key Features

Daily and Weekly Habits: Create custom habits with flexible scheduling, categories, colours, and time-of-day tags.
Virtual Pet System: Care for a pet that grows through three life stages as you earn XP from completed habits.
Mood Reflection: Pet expression changes (sad / normal / happy) based on daily habit completion.
Streak Tracking: Build daily and weekly streaks with a 30-day visual heatmap.
Accessories System: Unlock and equip hats, collars, and toys by reaching streak milestones.
Achievements: 13 achievements rewarding consistent habit-building.
Mood Journal: Log how you're feeling each day with optional notes and view your mood history.
Habit Guide: Built-in educational content on habit-building science and easy starter habits.
Wellness Tips: Curated stress relief, time management, and balance tips for students and workers.
User Profile: Personal stats, profile picture upload, and habit overview.
